01 Local, not generic
Why Caleb

Two counties that don't move the same.

Salt Lake and Utah County behave like different markets, and inside each one it can change street to street. What sells in eleven days in Sugar House can sit for two months in Eagle Mountain at the same price per foot.

You get pricing and strategy built on what's happening in your area right now — not a statewide average that describes nobody's house.
