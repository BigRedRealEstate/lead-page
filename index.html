Two counties that don't move the same.

Salt Lake and Utah County behave like dif­ferent markets, and inside each one it can change street to street. What sells in eleven days in Sugar House can sit for two months in Eagle Mountain at the same price per foot.

You get pricing and strategy built on what's hap­pen­ing in your area right now, not a statewide average that describes nobody's house.

Meet Caleb

I started in this business sweeping out houses.

I was born and raised in Riverton and graduated from Riverton High. Apart from three years in St. George, I have lived in Riverton, Herriman, or Lehi my whole life. My wife and I are raising our son and daughter here, and when I am not working I am outside, usually fishing.

My first job in this industry was rough clean for a home builder in 2016. I was sweep­ing sawdust out of houses that were still going up, and I got hooked on how a house actu­ally goes together. I got licen­sed after my son was born, went to work for a builder in southern Utah, and closed $18 million in volume in my first ten months and $26 million the year after.

Since then I have worked inside four dif­ferent buil­ders, so I know how they price, where they have room, and what they will give up to keep a deal together.
